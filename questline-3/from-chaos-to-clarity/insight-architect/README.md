# Insight Architect: Students Performance EDA

## Visualizations Overview
1. **Histogram (Math Scores):** Analyzes the distribution and frequency of student math scores.
2. **Bar Chart (Test Preparation Impact):** Compares average subject performance between students who completed test preparation vs. those who did not.
3. **Box Plot (Parental Level of Education):** Examines score distributions, medians, and spread across varying levels of parental education.
4. **Scatter Plot (Reading vs. Writing Scores):** Investigates the linear relationship between literacy skills separated by gender.
5. **Correlation Heatmap:** Quantifies pairwise linear relationships across math, reading, and writing metrics.

---

## Key Insights & Findings

1. **Near-Normal Score Distribution:** Math scores approximate a normal distribution centered around a mean of ~66, with a slight left skew due to isolated low-scoring outliers.
2. **Impact of Test Preparation:** Students who completed the test preparation course systematically outperformed those who did not across all three subjects (scoring 5–8 points higher on average).
3. **Writing Shows Greatest Prep Benefit:** The test preparation course created the most pronounced score improvement in the `writing score` category.
4. **Influence of Parental Education:** Higher parental education levels (e.g., Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees) correlate with higher median reading scores and fewer low-score anomalies compared to students whose parents only completed high school.
5. **Strong Literacy Correlation:** There is an exceptionally high positive correlation (~0.95) between reading scores and writing scores, indicating that strength in reading is a direct predictor of strong writing performance.
6. **Gender Performance Patterns:** Female students consistently show higher median scores in reading and writing, while male students demonstrate marginally higher median scores in mathematics.
